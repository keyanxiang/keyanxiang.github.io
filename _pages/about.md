I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the **National School of Development, Peking University**, with an expected graduation date of June 2027. My advisors are **Wei Huang** and **Xiaoyan Lei**.

My research interests are **Development Economics, Public Economics, and Health Economics**. My work studies how health, environmental conditions, education, and economic shocks shape household behavior, welfare, and human capital.

Before joining Peking University, I received my B.A. in Economics from **Southwestern University of Finance and Economics** in 2022.
