Welcome to my homepage!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the National School of Development, Peking University.

My research interests include development economics, public economics, and health economics. I primarily focus on how external shocks and public policies impact household behavior, welfare, and long-run human capital formation.

I am on the academic job market for the 2026–2027 academic year.
